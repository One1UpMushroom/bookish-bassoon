One light

Chartreuse marks what is live: titles, the axis letters, the selected step, Start and Resume, and the running state. Everything else is fog and steel.

Safety colours

Red means stop, abort or alarm. Amber means hold or door. Every state also says its name in words.

Controls follow the state

During a job the axis keys, Zero all and Home all give way to Abort, and jog locks. An alarm shows its reason next to Clear alarm.

Router speed

The big figure is the measured RPM. There is no sensor yet, so it shows dashes. “Set” is the S value FluidNC was told to use.

Fonts on the Pi

Oswald, Share Tech Mono and Barlow are installed on the Raspberry Pi, so they cost nothing on the Jackpot3's storage.
